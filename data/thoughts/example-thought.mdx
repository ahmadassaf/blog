This is an example thought - a shorter, more casual form of writing compared to full blog posts.

Thoughts are perfect for:
- Quick reflections and insights
- Short tutorials or tips
- Brief updates
- Ideas you want to share without a full article

## How to Use Thoughts

Simply create a new `.mdx` file in the `data/thoughts/` directory with the following frontmatter:

```yaml
---
title: 'Your Thought Title'
date: '2025-03-10'
tags: ['tag1', 'tag2']
draft: false
---
```

Then write your content using Markdown or MDX. You can use all the same components available in blog posts.

## Keeping It Simple

The beauty of thoughts is that they don't need to be polished, long-form content. Share what's on your mind!
