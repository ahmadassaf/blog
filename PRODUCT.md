## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

Ahmad Assaf's personal site publishes his writing, projects and publications. The Startup CTO’s Handbook is a living reference within the blog, with an overview, eight ordered chapters and dated change histories.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Keep the handbook connected to the blog while giving its overview and chapters a distinct presentation.
- Readers can start at the beginning, choose a chapter, or catch up on changes.
- Chapter order is independent of the date of the latest update.
- Authored chapter changelogs are the single source for both chapter history and the combined updates view.
- Preserve existing article URLs, prose, citations, MDX components and frontmatter. In-page contents remain collapsed unless explicitly enabled.
- Verify phone, tablet, laptop and wide desktop layouts, including nearby breakpoints; support the site's light and dark themes.
- Work in the original checkout. Do not push these changes.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the site's existing Plus Jakarta Sans typography, blue accent, neutral surfaces and personal editorial voice. The handbook's identity comes from a stronger title treatment, numbered chapter directory and a dedicated reading layout.

## Evidence on Hand

The handbook overview and eight chapters live in `data/blog/management/cto-handbook*.mdx`. Each has a title, subtitle, dates, series order and an authored changelog. Gaudi supplies the existing site primitives and design tokens.
