80% of bugs can be found by:
1. Reading the error message carefully
2. Checking the most recent changes
3. Using `console.log` (or equivalent) at strategic points

The other 20% take 80% of your debugging time.

## The Last 20%

These are the bugs that:
- Only happen in production
- Depend on timing or race conditions
- Are caused by assumptions you didn't know you made
- Stem from external dependencies

For these, you need different tools: proper logging, monitoring, reproduction steps, and sometimes just patience.

## A Debugging Strategy

Before diving deep:
1. Can you reproduce it reliably?
2. What changed recently?
3. What are you assuming?

Question 3 is the most important. Your assumptions are usually where the bug is hiding.
